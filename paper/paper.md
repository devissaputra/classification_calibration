# Probability Calibration Under Class Imbalance: A Reproducible Study on UCI Bank Marketing

## Status
Research-bundle manuscript scaffold. Numerical results are intentionally omitted until regenerated by the real-data runner.

## Research question
How do uncalibrated logistic regression, sigmoid calibration and isotonic calibration differ in discrimination and probability quality on an untouched holdout sample of UCI Bank Marketing?

## Data
UCI Bank Marketing (dataset 222), DOI 10.24432/C5K306, CC BY 4.0.

## Design
A fixed stratified 80/20 split is created with seed 42. Mixed-type preprocessing is fitted inside the training pipeline. Calibrators use five-fold cross-validation within training data. The final test set remains untouched until evaluation.

## Outcomes
ROC-AUC measures ranking. Brier score and log loss measure probabilistic quality. ECE-10 is reported as a binned calibration diagnostic and is interpreted with its known binning sensitivity.

## Validity
The study is a methodological benchmark on historical marketing data. It does not establish current population validity, causal effects, or suitability for consequential banking decisions.

## Reproducibility
The executable protocol is `src/run_experiment.py`; machine-readable outputs are written to `results/metrics.json`.
